Welcome to my blog. This is a space for the ideas, questions, and small discoveries that come with building things.

## What I’ll write about

- **Data engineering:** moving from raw data to useful, reliable systems.
- **Machine learning:** the work around a model, as well as the model itself.
- **Software:** tools, experiments, and lessons worth keeping.

Some posts will be short notes. Others will be longer explanations with code and examples. The goal is the same: leave a clearer trail from a question to an answer.

## Start small, keep learning

> A useful note doesn’t have to be the final word on a subject.
